This browser game adapts the traditional rules into a one-player format: you attack, rebuild, dodge, and defend against a CPU team.

How to Play

  • On your attack turn, drag from the ball and release to throw at the stone stack.
  • You get up to 3 throws to knock the stack down.
  • After a successful hit, move with WASD or arrow keys to collect stones and bring them back.
  • On mobile, drag on the field to throw, and use the joystick to move while rebuilding.
  • The round is won immediately when either side rebuilds all 7 stones into the pillar.
  • Higher levels add obstacles that can block throws, create ricochets, and give you cover to hide behind.
  • The CPU throws from fixed spots and can only knock you out by hitting below the waist.
  • When the CPU attacks, click to throw from your marked position and stop its runners before they rebuild.
